Help

How do I upload a file?

Create an account, sign in, then click “Upload a file” on the home page.

Do I need an account to download?

No. Anyone can browse and open resources. You only need an account to upload.

Who manages the site?

Only the confirmed admin and authorized admins can manage the platform.
